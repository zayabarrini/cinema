## 📝 How to Structure Your Own Learning TOC

If you want a Korean-style roadmap, follow this order:

1. **Master the 3 Ending Styles** (해 / 해요 / 합니다).
2. **Learn the 7 Major Irregulars** (ㄷ, ㅂ, ㅅ, 르, ㅎ, ㄹ, 으).
3. **Learn Connective Endings** (Because, If, When, But).
4. **Learn Noun Modifiers** (Present, Past, Future).
5. **Learn Nominalization** (-기, -음).
6. **Learn Advanced Voices** (Passive & Causative).
