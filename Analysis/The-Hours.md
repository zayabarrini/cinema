### Order of Scenes in *The Hours* (2002)

**1. Opening – Virginia Woolf’s Suicide (1941)**
- **People:** Virginia Woolf, Leonard Woolf
- **Description:** Virginia writes a farewell letter to Leonard, puts on her coat, fills her pockets with stones, and walks into the River Ouse, where she drowns.

**2. Morning – Clarissa Vaughan (New York, Present Day)**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Sally (her partner), Julia (her daughter)
- **Description:** Clarissa leaves her apartment to buy flowers for a party she is throwing that evening to celebrate her friend Richard, a poet who has just won a major literary award.

**3. Morning – Laura Brown (Los Angeles, 1951)**
- **People:** Laura Brown, Dan (her husband), Richie (her young son)
- **Description:** Laura is pregnant and preparing a birthday cake for Dan. She struggles with a sense of emptiness and reads *Mrs. Dalloway*.

**4. Clarissa at the Florist – Meeting Walter Hardy**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Walter Hardy (a friend with AIDS)
- **Description:** Clarissa buys flowers and invites Walter to the party. He asks if Richard’s novel is about her.

**5. Clarissa Visits Richard**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Richard (her former lover, now dying of AIDS)
- **Description:** Richard is in a dark, shabby apartment. Clarissa tries to convince him to come to her party. He is depressed and mocking, and they reminisce about a kiss on a beach years ago.

**6. Virginia at Home – The Household (Richmond, 1923)**
- **People:** Virginia Woolf, Leonard Woolf, Nelly (the cook)
- **Description:** Virginia is writing *Mrs. Dalloway*. She argues with Nelly about meals and with Leonard about her health, doctors, and her desire to return to London.

**7. Laura Baking the Cake**
- **People:** Laura Brown, Richie
- **Description:** Laura and Richie bake a cake for Dan’s birthday. Laura feels insecure and overwhelmed, even by this simple task.

**8. Clarissa and Sally – Preparing for the Party**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Sally, Julia
- **Description:** Clarissa receives a phone call from Sally, who is out. They discuss the party arrangements and joke about seating ex-lovers. Clarissa is anxious.

**9. Laura and Kitty – A Visit**
- **People:** Laura Brown, Kitty (her neighbor)
- **Description:** Kitty comes over, visibly upset. She reveals she must go to the hospital for a procedure on a growth in her uterus. She envies Laura for being pregnant. Laura comforts her.

**10. Virginia’s Sister Vanessa Visits**
- **People:** Virginia Woolf, Vanessa Bell (her sister), Vanessa’s children (Julian, Quentin, Angelica)
- **Description:** Vanessa and her children arrive. They find a dead bird and have a small funeral for it. Virginia has a moment where she seems distant and describes her thoughts on killing a character in her novel.

**11. Laura Leaves Richie with a Neighbor**
- **People:** Laura Brown, Richie, Mrs. Latch (neighbor)
- **Description:** Laura leaves Richie with Mrs. Latch to “run an errand.” Richie cries and doesn’t want her to go, but she leaves anyway, driving away in her car.

**12. Clarissa and Louis Waters**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Louis Waters (Richard’s former lover)
- **Description:** Louis arrives early for the party. They have an intense, emotional conversation about Richard, their shared past, and Louis’s new life in San Francisco. Clarissa becomes overwhelmed and cries.

**13. Laura at a Hotel**
- **People:** Laura Brown (alone)
- **Description:** Laura checks into a hotel room. She lies on the bed, reads *Mrs. Dalloway*, and has a vision of drowning. She decides not to go through with her plan to kill herself.

**14. Virginia Saying Goodbye to Vanessa**
- **People:** Virginia Woolf, Vanessa Bell, Leonard Woolf
- **Description:** Vanessa and her children leave. Virginia pleads with her sister, asking if she thinks she (Virginia) might ever escape Richmond. She looks out at the train and seems lost.

**15. Clarissa and Julia – The Truth About Richard**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Julia (her daughter)
- **Description:** Clarissa is dressing for the party. Julia asks her about her life and whether she is happy. Clarissa confesses that Richard makes her feel that her life is trivial, and recalls a moment of pure happiness from her youth.

**16. Virginia and Leonard – The Argument**
- **People:** Virginia Woolf, Leonard Woolf
- **Description:** A fierce argument erupts when Virginia takes a walk without telling anyone. Virginia accuses Leonard and the doctors of imprisoning her, saying she would choose death over Richmond. Leonard finally agrees to move back to London.

**17. Laura Returns to Pick Up Richie**
- **People:** Laura Brown, Richie, Mrs. Latch
- **Description:** Laura returns to get Richie. She is distant and quiet. She tells him she loves him, then drives him home.

**18. Clarissa Returns to Richard – His Suicide**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Richard
- **Description:** Clarissa finds Richard standing at an open window, having removed the glass. He tells her he loves her but cannot face the party and the rest of the day. He says, “I’m afraid I can’t make it to the party,” and then falls out the window to his death. Clarissa is devastated.

**19. Laura’s Birthday Dinner**
- **People:** Laura Brown, Dan, Richie
- **Description:** Dan comes home. He loves the cake. Dan tells a story about thinking of a shy girl named Laura McGrath when he was in the war, and how that thought kept him going. Laura seems troubled.

**20. Virginia Writing the Ending**
- **People:** Virginia Woolf (alone, writing)
- **Description:** Virginia writes the final pages of *Mrs. Dalloway*, deciding the fate of her heroine and her own reasons for needing to kill off the poet (Septimus) so that others value life more. Leonard comes in to say goodnight.

**21. The Party – Laura Brown Arrives**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Sally, Julia, Louis Waters, Laura Brown (now an old woman)
- **Description:** Richard’s party is happening. Laura Brown (Richard’s mother, now elderly) arrives unexpectedly. Clarissa is shocked. They sit together in the bedroom, away from the party.

**22. Clarissa and Laura – The Final Conversation**
- **People:** Clarissa Vaughan, Laura Brown
- **Description:** Laura Brown explains that she left her two children when they were young. She went to a hotel to plan her suicide but instead chose life, moving to Canada to work in a library. She says she cannot be forgiven. Clarissa sees that she is Richard’s mother and that Richard’s novel was about her.

**23. Closing – Virginia’s Final Letter and Death**
- **People:** Virginia Woolf (voiceover), Leonard Woolf
- **Description:** Virginia’s voice reads her suicide letter to Leonard as she walks into the river and drowns. The film ends with her words: “Always the years between us. Always the years. Always the love. Always the hours.”
